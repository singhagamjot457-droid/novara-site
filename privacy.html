Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information we may collect

Novara may collect information needed to provide the learning service, such as account information, selected class level, study progress, quiz activity and support messages.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to provide the app, save progress, improve learning features, operate paid plans, prevent misuse and respond to support requests.

3. Payments

Payment details should be processed by the selected payment provider. Novara should not store full card numbers, CVV values or banking credentials in the app.

4. Children's privacy

Novara is intended for students, including minors. A parent or guardian should be involved in account creation, payment and any information that requires adult consent. We aim to collect only information needed to provide the service.

5. Data storage

Depending on the final deployment, some study progress may be stored locally in the browser or through a secure backend.
