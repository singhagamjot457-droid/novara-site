Frequently asked questions

A few basics about Novara.

Which classes does Novara support?

Novara is designed for Classes 3 through 12, with class-specific question banks.

Which subjects are included?

Mathematics, Science, History, Geography, English, Reasoning, Computer Science and General Knowledge.

Is Standard free?

Yes. Standard is intended to provide the core Novara learning experience without requiring a paid plan.

How does the Tutor work?

The app includes a study tutor designed to explain school questions and concepts step by step. Cloud AI and advanced backend features can be connected separately.

How do I contact Novara?

Use the contact page for launch-stage support and account questions.
